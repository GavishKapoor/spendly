Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and how we keep your financial data safe.

Last updated: November 2026
01

Data We Collect

We only collect what we need to run Spendly for you:

  • Account details — your name, email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense records — amounts, categories, dates and descriptions you enter.
  • Basic technical data — such as browser type and login times, used to keep your account secure.

We do not collect bank credentials, card numbers or access to your financial accounts.

02

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide and improve Spendly — showing your expenses, building category breakdowns and monthly summaries, and keeping your account secure. We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

03

Data Storage

Your data is stored on secured servers and is only accessible to you through your account. Passwords are never stored in plain text. When you delete an expense or your account, the related data is permanently removed from our active systems.

04

Third Party Services

We may rely on a small number of trusted providers — for example, hosting and font delivery — to operate Spendly. These providers only receive the minimum information needed to perform their service and are not permitted to use it for any other purpose.

05

Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach us at privacy@spendly.example and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.
